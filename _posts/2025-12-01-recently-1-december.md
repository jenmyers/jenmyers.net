### Links

- "Art is interesting precisely _because_ a human made it, through a long, difficult process that could be unique only to them. The human story behind the art is just as much a part of the work as the paint or the notes or the words or any other part of the medium." [On alchemy and "AI."](https://joshcollinsworth.com/blog/alchemy)

- [This interview with Sissy Spacek about one of my favorite films, Robert Altman's _3 Women_, is full of beauty and wonder.](https://www.vulture.com/article/sissy-spacek-answers-every-question-we-have-about-3-women.html) 

- Today I learned that [_Casino_ belongs in the Chicago film canon](https://chicagoreader.com/film-tv/movie-feature/casino-martin-scorsese-nicholas-pileggi-mob-mafia/).

- One week ago, I had the tremendous good fortune to attend a screening of [_It Was Just an Accident_](https://letterboxd.com/film/it-was-just-an-accident/) (incredible film) with its filmmaker, Jafar Panahi, on his first US tour. I read today that Iran, which has previously imprisoned Panahi twice and confined him to house arrest for making films outside their censorship system, has sentenced him yet again, in absentia, to another prison term. They haven't stopped him from making films, though. [This video of Panahi's New York stop and interview with Martin Scorsese is worth watching and appreciating.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1tav1MUblM4)

- [On letter writing](https://www.wbur.org/cognoscenti/2025/11/14/writing-letters-postcards-mail-laura-mctaggart), which is on my list of habits to return to in the coming year. Open to recipient recommendations.

- "If reality is this fluid, and if the mind communes with matter in ways we don’t understand, maybe miracles aren’t miracles. Intellect simply hasn’t caught up with—or recovered—intuition." [The tune of things.](https://harpers.org/archive/2025/12/the-tune-of-things-christian-wiman-consciousness-god/)

### Reading/Watching/Listening

- Of course I went to see the new [Rian Johnson/Benoit Blanc joint](https://letterboxd.com/film/wake-up-dead-man/) in the theater as soon as I possibly could. My favorite of the Blanc series so far. Lovely cinematography—Steve Yedlin proves a film produced by a streamer can still look great. Go see this during its all-too-brief run in theaters to send a message to Netflix about their lack of faith in the theatrical experience.

- So, Oz Perkins's [_Keeper_](https://letterboxd.com/film/keeper-2025/). A lesser effort, not weird enough to make the true believers happy or to convince the Perkins skeptics otherwise—but I still liked it. For what it's worth.

- I haven't been reading a lot worth mentioning lately. Recommend something good to me.

- Inspired by _Wake Up Dead Man_, have some Tom Waits.
