"In the shedding what remains is honor. We evolve, we falter, we learn from our transgressions, and then repeat them. We plunge back into the abyss we labored to exit and find ourselves within another turn of the wheel. And then having found the fortitude to do so, we begin the excruciating yet exquisite process of letting go. There is a brilliant calm, akin to natural light. All about us is debris, and yet we step lightly so not to tread a waning silhouette, our own primordial skin."

— Patti Smith, <em>Bread of Angels</em>
