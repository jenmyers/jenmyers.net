[Daydreamers and sleepwalkers: crossing the borderlands of the unconscious.](https://thereader.mitpress.mit.edu/daydreamers-and-sleepwalkers-crossing-the-borderlands-of-the-unconscious/)

### Reading/Watching/Listening

- While I prefer used book stores, the odder the better, for my book shopping, every once in a while I like to hit a Barnes & Noble like I'm a teenager again and roam blindly to see what I can find. Last time I did that, I found [_Silver in the Wood_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/f99f7b4c-0dde-49ac-8f7f-bc7c2ddfd2f7) by Emily Tesh, a slender but deeply rooted folk tale that is a delight to read. Queuing up the second in the duology now.

- As is evident to anyone who's been around here for more than a minute, I've been reading Nick Cave's [The Red Hand Files](https://www.theredhandfiles.com) for a long time, but I just got around to his book of conversations with Sean O'Hagan, [_Faith, Hope and Carnage_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/e166085a-9972-46d7-8ecb-fe2dfd95acc3). Highly recommended to those who like Nick Cave, the Red Hand Files, or just the process of reflection, growth and poetics.

- I wanted to read at least one of the "Murderbot" books before watching the television adaptation, so I read Martha Wells's [_All Systems Red_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/54db2d05-33c5-4c7c-88be-f7454d801dea) and then I watched the first season of [_Murderbot_](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/murderbot/umc.cmc.5owrzntj9v1gpg31wshflud03) and I enjoyed both thoroughly.

- Finally caught up with the latest Park Chan-wook film, [_No Other Choice_](https://letterboxd.com/film/no-other-choice-2025/), which, unsurprisingly, is terrific. If I had got to it sooner, it would have been on my [favorites of 2025 list](https://jenmyers.net/notes/favorite-films-of-2025.html).

- A couple of films that I feel have been either overlooked or too-harshly critiqued: [_Timestalker_](https://letterboxd.com/film/timestalker/), director Alice Lowe's tongue-in-cheek, candy-colored, time-traveling take on always falling in love with the wrong man, which I found sweet and fun and lovely; and [_Pretty Thing_](https://letterboxd.com/film/pretty-thing/), a twist on a stalker story that twists back around through more emotional complexity than I think most people are giving it credit for (also why isn't the Alicia Silverstone comeback taking full hold, she deserves it, we all deserve it).

- The other day, [Courtney Love posted a new photo of her and Melissa Auf der Maur drinking tea together](https://www.instagram.com/p/DUE0dq2CelK/?img_index=1) and maybe it was just the 90s-teenage-girl-turned-40-something-woman thing, but made me unexpectedly emotional. We made it through. We all somehow survived. Who could have guessed.
