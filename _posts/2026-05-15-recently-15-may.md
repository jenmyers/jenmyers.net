### Links

- "So every time you share a link on your blog, every time you write a few sentences about why someone else’s work matters to you, every time you add a new entry to a blogroll or a links page – you are a curator. You are doing what no algorithm can do. You’re saying: I am a person. I read this. I think you should read it too." [On curation.](https://newsletter.ownyourweb.site/archive/own-your-web-issue-18-curators/)

- [The internet has no benches.](https://news.spencer.place/p/the-internet-has-no-benches)

- Humans weren’t given souls by God or genes. We made them ourselves with language–[turning sentience into something sacred](https://aeon.co/essays/you-know-what-consciousness-is-you-live-in-soul-land).

- [What is the chance of a message in a bottle being found?](https://theconversation.com/what-is-the-chance-of-a-message-in-a-bottle-being-found-272122) Spoiler alert: not good. I still think I could do it, though.

- "People put stuff off, like the angry guy who didn’t climb the mountain when he was 20 because there wasn’t time. No, you didn’t make time. Any project… 'One day, I’m gonna write that novel.' Pal? You better start tomorrow morning because the right time never happens. It’s when you boldly determine it." Sometimes, you just need [Henry Rollins to set you straight](https://thecreativeindependent.com/people/henry-rollins-on-defining-success/), you know?

### Reading/Watching/Listening

- I was anticipating the new film from director Damian McCarthy and [_Hokum_](https://letterboxd.com/film/hokum/) did not disappoint. A weird, grungy, ghostly blast.

- I rather blindly started reading Brian Evenson's [_Last Days_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/c120c213-5afb-4061-9cef-62f0cda4bbda) and whoa. I did not properly know what I was getting myself into. Disturbing and astonishingly good.

- [_Widow's Bay_](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/widows-bay/umc.cmc.1zzly0vah46bnvnwf0qkrjhh2) rules. I hope they make a million seasons of it.

- A couple of weeks ago, I got to see Joe Hisaishi in concert. What a lovely experience.
