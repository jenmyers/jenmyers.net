I'm so glad someone came up with the idea of reflection on the occasion of a new calendar year, because otherwise I would have to invent justifications for one of my favorite hobbies: making lists. Here's one to recap notable things liked and done in 2025.

- [I read 68 books](https://app.thestorygraph.com/wrap-up/2025/jenmyers). Standouts were: [_Hungerstone_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/40fa01e4-4914-46dc-a51c-de059690f4dc), Kat Dunn; [_El Dorado Drive_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/2dad9f8d-841a-4b0e-b903-f4a56e935fa5), Megan Abbott; [_Witchcraft for Wayward Girls_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/290a13de-3ecf-4b8e-b9d0-48b1f06c92bf), Grady Hendrix; [_I Who Have Never Known Men_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/3e75ad1a-3361-4fc1-9f7c-f0d8ace60cf1), Jacqueline Harpman; [_The Tenant_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/bd5592c0-1535-4d08-95ee-c84c98bcf300), Roland Topor; [_Wide Sargasso Sea_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/0d1a7b0b-c58b-49c7-92b8-444f4f35a9ab), Jean Rhys; [_The Prime of Miss Jean Brodie_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/1c9003a8-8be9-4beb-b22a-e3b89af55097), Muriel Spark; [_Generation Loss_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/663d5fc3-03bb-4491-a37d-f5ec291ad81b), Elizabeth Hand; [_Tattoos: The Untold History of a Modern Art_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/2b8f1902-4004-42da-831e-7b5d245586db), Matt Lodder; [_Miss May Does Not Exist: The Life and Work of Elaine May, Hollywood's Hidden Genius_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/eac3fdfa-3bf4-46cc-94f6-604b6076ea61), Carrie Courogen.

- I watched 299 movies (that's totally normal, shut up). [All my favorite ones are over in their own post.](https://jenmyers.net/notes/favorite-films-of-2025.html)

- In TV news, I, like I think everyone else, liked _Pluribus_. Most notably in this arena, however, I rewatched _The Americans_ for the first time since its airing and turns out it’s still incredible, just in case you were wondering.

- I co-hosted and released fifteen episodes of my little horror film podcast, [Quiet Little Horrors](https://quietlittlehorrors.com/). My favorite episodes: [_Heavenly Creatures_](https://quietlittlehorrors.com/episode-06-10-heavenly-creatures/), [_Fréwaka_](https://quietlittlehorrors.com/episode-06-07-frewaka/) and [Our Favorite Vampires](https://quietlittlehorrors.com/episode-06-02-our-favorite-vampires/).

- I went to five opera performances: _La Bohème_, _The Listeners_ (my first contemporary opera), _Medea_, and _Cavalleria rusticana & Pagliacci_ at Chicago Lyric Opera, and _Die Walküre_ (my first Ring cycle opera) at Santa Fe Opera. Plus, a bonus _Bluebeard’s Castle_ (a particular favorite of mine) performed by Chicago Symphony Orchestra, with one of my favorite singers, [Christian Van Horn](https://christianvanhorn.com/).

- Speaking of, I went to four CSO performances. Standout: Dvořák's Symphony No. 9 (_From the New World_), conducted by Riccardo Muti.

- I got three new tattoos, including a relatively large cover-up. Shout out to [Jennifer Trok](https://jennifertrok.xyz/) at Deluxe Tattoo in Chicago for my Bride.

- In the spring, I took a train to New Orleans, where I watched a bunch of horror movies, bought books in old bookstores, contemplated mortality in cemeteries, consumed a tremendous amount of coffee and beignets, and wandered around one of my favorite cities in the world. In the summer, I took a train to Santa Fe, where I looked at art, went to the opera, hiked in the hills, got lost in [Meow Wolf](https://meowwolf.com/visit/santa-fe), bought books in slightly newer bookstores, and listened to an astrologer describe the focus of my coming years.

- On the latter trip, [I made a train video](https://vimeo.com/1106913575).

- I also traveled a lot, not by train, for work and family purposes: Atlanta, Orlando (x2), Charlotte, São Paulo, and Pittsburgh. I did not buy any books on those trips—but I did, however, get a new baby nephew during one of them, so that’s pretty neat.

- 2025 was an active and people-filled year. Which is great, but in the coming year I’m going for a slightly better balance and some more time to take care of myself and make more things.
