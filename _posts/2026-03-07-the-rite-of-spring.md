On Friday, I went to hear the [Chicago Symphony Orchestra, conducted by Klaus Mäkelä](https://cso.org/performances/25-26/cso-featured-concerts/makela-conducts-the-rite-of-spring/) perform an inspired program: _Le Bœuf sur le toit_, _An American in Paris_, and _The Rite of Spring_. After a demoralizing week of confrontation with the worst sort of modern obsession with mechanized production for production's sake, it was a particularly reaffirming artistic experience. The orchestra was on fire and Mäkelä (CSO's new wunderkind music director, whom I had never seen conduct before) is a joy to watch. The whole house was on their feet for four straight ovations afterwards. So, a reminder, should you need it, that humans and the things they make, and share with each other with honesty and enthusiasm, are still pretty great and valuable.
