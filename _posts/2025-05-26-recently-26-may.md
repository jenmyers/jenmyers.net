### Reading/Watching/Listening

- I finally found my way to Elizabeth Hand’s [Cass Neary books](https://app.thestorygraph.com/series/1879) and of course I’m always here for a great antiheroine.

- I’ve also been reading the Perry Mason novels. Turns out they’re pretty fun. In case you were wondering.

- On the heavier side, I’m still working my way through Eugene Thacker’s “Horror of Philosophy” trilogy and am almost finished with [_Starry Speculative Corpse_](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/ad7b178e-83d0-4ce3-8ca8-fcfac0c0fe6c). I love this stuff.

- I haven’t watched many new films that I feel like mentioning, but I have entered an era of rewatching films, especially some favorites I’ve managed to see in the theater for the first time. Highlights: _Johnny Guitar_, _Sweet Smell of Success_, _Persona_ and _Picnic at Hanging Rock_. Rewatching great movies: recommended.

- Peter Murphy has a new album out. Which is all I think needs to be said.
