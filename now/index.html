This is an overview of what's going in my life right now, with more details than usually make it to my website or social media. Inspired by Nownownow.

I work as an engineering manager at Nubank, where I manage the developer success team for Datomic. These days, I am often out and about at tech conferences spreading the good Datomic word.

A few years ago, I went back to school to make some use of all the credits I acquired in my first, unfinished and long-neglected collegiate attempt, as well as shift my degree focus to something I actually enjoy. I am now on track to complete my undergraduate degree in film and media studies by the end of 2027.

I have also been building my practical filmmaking skills and am currently a part of the Firehouse Film Collective. Hoping to shoot my first short film in the coming year.

I bought a home on the far northside of the best city in the world, where I spend an inordinate amount of time at the edge of Lake Michigan. I do a lot of long-distance walking and yoga, although not at the exact same time. Otherwise, I go to movies, the opera, the symphony, the odd rock 'n' roll show, and other cities/countries as the fancy takes me.
